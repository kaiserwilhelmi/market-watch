How signals work

Each series is converted to its cost impact: for prices, a rise means higher costs; for currencies (foreign units per dollar), a rise means a stronger dollar and cheaper imports. Signals use the 3-month and 12-month cost impact:

Order of checks: Alert first, then Opportunity (a sharp recent drop outranks an older 12-month rise), then Watch, otherwise Stable. Producer price indexes are published with a lag of about one to two months, so "latest" shows the most recent month available.

Updates: a scheduled job downloads fresh data from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) every morning, recalculates every signal and republishes this page.

Sources

    Data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (producer price indexes), U.S. Energy Information Administration (diesel), Federal Reserve Board (exchange rates), via FRED. Signals and actions are illustrative guidance, not financial advice.
